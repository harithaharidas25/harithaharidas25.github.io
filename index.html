Professional Insights

Five red flags

Unexpected activity, unexplained third-party payments, inconsistent documentation, unusual counterparties and unexplained changes in behaviour warrant contextual review.

Source of funds vs source of wealth

Source of funds concerns the origin of particular funds; source of wealth concerns how overall wealth was accumulated.

Suspicious transactions

Monitoring alerts are starting points. Compare behaviour, gather evidence and document a defensible decision.

AML in New Zealand

Risk-based due diligence, ongoing monitoring and reporting obligations form part of the local framework.

Fraud prevention and customer experience
